Type it. Draw it. Same note.

Jotdown is a note-taking app where writing and sketching flow into each other, with no switching modes and no separate canvas.

Open web editor Download for Windows

Free. Runs in any modern browser, or as an app on Windows.

Notes that don't make you choose

  • Typing and drawing together

    Sketch between paragraphs, circle a line, or draw an arrow, all in the same flow as your text.

  • Made for a pen

    Works with a stylus on a laptop, tablet, or phone, and just as well with a mouse and keyboard.

  • Your notes are plain files

    The Windows app saves notes as .jdn files and lets you open a whole folder of them in a built-in notes browser.

  • One app, two ways to use it

    Start in the browser without installing anything, or get the Windows app for auto-save to files, tabs, and full-screen focus.
